The person behind the business

Knowledge from both sides of the industry.

Davis Pest Solutions was founded by EJ, an Auckland-based licensed pest technician with years of industry experience.

Before working hands-on in the field, EJ spent years on the supplier side, building knowledge of professional pest-control products, equipment and application methods. Combined with field work, this supports a practical approach: first understand the pest and the setting, then select and apply an appropriate solution responsibly.

Coming from a Filipino background and being part of the Auckland community inspired the Davis tagline: Your Filo Pestie.

Our symbol

Why the Tuatara?

The tuatara is a unique part of Aotearoa New Zealand and holds cultural significance for Māori.

Some iwi regard the tuatara as a kaitiaki — a guardian of knowledge.

Inspired by this idea of guardianship, the tuatara represents our commitment to protecting the places that matter most: your home, your business and your peace of mind.
