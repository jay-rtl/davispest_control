What happens during pest treatment?

The process normally includes discussing what you have noticed, assessing the pest problem and property, recommending an approach, carrying out the agreed treatment and providing aftercare advice.

How should I prepare?

Preparation varies. Depending on the job, you may be asked to make areas accessible, move or cover items, clean specific spaces or manage pets. Follow the instructions provided for your treatment.

How long does treatment take?

Timing depends on the pest, property size, access, activity level and treatment method. EJ can give more useful guidance after learning about the property.

Are treatments suitable around children and pets?

Treatment planning considers occupants, children, pets and the environment. Share all relevant details and follow the preparation and re-entry instructions supplied for the selected treatment.

When can I return to a treated area?

There is no single re-entry period for every treatment. Follow the job-specific instructions EJ gives you, as they depend on the products, methods and area treated.

What is different about residential and commercial treatment?

Home treatments focus on household activity, occupants and living areas. Commercial plans may also need to account for operations, customers, staff, site access, scheduling and ongoing prevention.

Do you offer ongoing pest management?

Yes. Ongoing pest management and maintenance programs are available for commercial sites needing scheduled service and a prevention focus.

How do I get a quote?

Call 02047-DAVIS or use the ServiceM8 online quote request. The more context you provide, the easier it is to recommend the next step.
