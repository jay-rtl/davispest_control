Understand the signs

What to look for

Scratching, droppings, gnaw marks, nesting material and disturbed stored food are common reasons to request an inspection.

If you are unsure what you are seeing, describe the signs in your request. Avoid handling a pest or suspected nest to identify it.

The Davis approach

Assess first. Then plan.

Site assessment

Describe where you hear or see activity and whether it is inside, outside or near stored food. The assessment can then focus on access, shelter and food sources.

Treatment and aftercare

After discussing the findings, Davis recommends an approach suited to the pest, property and occupants. Treatment and follow-up needs are explained for the work agreed.

Safety and preparation

Keep children and pets away from any control equipment as instructed. The appropriate placement, monitoring and proofing advice depend on the site.

A useful answer

Will sealing a gap solve the problem on its own?

Proofing can be important, but an active problem may need assessment and control as well. Davis can advise on the sequence after looking at the property.
