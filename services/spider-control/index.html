Spider control in Auckland

Spider management depends on the species, the activity and the property. Davis reviews the places spiders shelter and what may be attracting their prey.

Understand the signs

What to look for

Recurring webbing, spiders around building edges or repeated activity in the same places can help locate the problem.

If you are unsure what you are seeing, describe the signs in your request. Avoid handling a pest or suspected nest to identify it.

The Davis approach

Assess first. Then plan.

Site assessment

Point out recurring webs and the places where you have seen spiders. An inspection can consider building edges, sheltered spaces and associated insect activity.

Treatment and aftercare

After discussing the findings, Davis recommends an approach suited to the pest, property and occupants. Treatment and follow-up needs are explained for the work agreed.

Safety and preparation

Advice on cleaning, exclusion and any treatment is specific to the site. Tell Davis about pets and areas that need special care.

A useful answer

Will removing webs stop spiders returning?

Removing webs helps with housekeeping, but recurring activity may need a closer look at shelter and prey around the property.
