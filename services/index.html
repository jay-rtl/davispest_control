Pest control services across Auckland.

Every pest problem is different. Davis looks beyond the pest you can see to understand where it is hiding, what is attracting it and the right way to deal with it.

Cockroach Control ↗

Seeing one? There are probably more hiding.

Cockroaches love warm, dark and hidden spaces. We look beyond the ones you can see and identify where they are hiding, what is attracting them and how they are getting in.

Treatment may involve targeted applications, professional baits and other appropriate control methods depending on the situation.

Davis Tip: Don't just spray the one you see—you may only be treating the visible part of the problem.

Rat & Mouse Control ↗

They Found a Way In. We’ll Find a Way Out.

We look for signs of activity, entry points, nesting areas and food sources before deciding on the right treatment approach.

Depending on the property, this may include strategically placed rodent stations, monitoring and recommendations to help prevent them from returning.

Davis Tip: Scratching at night, droppings or gnaw marks? Don't wait for your unwanted tenant to settle in.

Ant Control ↗

The ants you see are usually only part of the problem.

A visible trail can lead back to a much larger colony. We identify where they are travelling, what is attracting them and where the colony may be located.

Depending on the situation, targeted baiting and other appropriate control methods may be used.

Davis Tip: Don't destroy the ant trail before treatment—it can help us find the source.

Fly Control ↗

If flies keep coming back, there is usually a reason.

Killing adult flies may provide temporary relief, but recurring fly problems can point to an underlying breeding or attraction source.

We look at areas such as waste, food residue, moisture and drains to help address the problem at its source.

Davis Tip: If you're killing flies every day, it is probably time to find out why they keep coming back.

Wasp Control ↗

Found a wasp nest? Don't knock on the door.

Wasps can become aggressive when their nest is disturbed. We assess the nest location and activity before choosing an appropriate treatment approach.

Our priority is effective control while minimising unnecessary risk to people and pets nearby.

Davis Tip: You don't need to get close enough to count the wasps. Leave that part to us.

Flea Treatment ↗

Fleas don't just live on the pet.

Flea problems can involve eggs and developing stages throughout the surrounding environment, including carpets, furniture and areas where pets spend time.

Treatment focuses on the environment as well as the source of the infestation, with preparation and aftercare playing an important role.

Davis Tip: Seeing some flea activity after treatment doesn't always mean failure—the flea life cycle matters.

Spider Control ↗

You don't have to become friends with the spiders in your house.

Rather than simply chasing individual spiders, we look at where they are hiding, where webs are forming and what may be encouraging activity around your property.

Treatment can focus on exterior areas and common harbourage points to help reduce activity.

Davis Tip: If webs keep appearing in the same places, those areas are telling us where to look.

Residential Pest Control

Your home should feel like home—not a pest hotel.

Whether it is cockroaches, ants, spiders, rodents or a combination of pests, we assess the property and recommend a treatment based on what is actually happening.

We consider the pest, the property, activity levels and the people and pets around the treatment area.

Davis Tip: The cheapest treatment isn't always the cheapest solution if the same problem keeps coming back.

For businesses, pest control is about more than pests.

A pest problem can affect your customers, staff, food safety and reputation. We assess the site and develop a practical treatment and monitoring approach suited to your business.

Regular servicing, monitoring and preventative recommendations can help keep small problems from becoming big ones.

Davis Tip: The best time to discover a pest problem isn't when your customer does.
