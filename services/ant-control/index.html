Ant control in Auckland

Understand the signs

What to look for

Recurring trails, activity around food or ants entering through gaps can point to a wider problem.

If you are unsure what you are seeing, describe the signs in your request. Avoid handling a pest or suspected nest to identify it.

The Davis approach

Assess first. Then plan.

Site assessment

Note where ants appear, when the trail is active and whether the activity returns after cleaning. Those details help focus the inspection.

Treatment and aftercare

After discussing the findings, Davis recommends an approach suited to the pest, property and occupants. Treatment and follow-up needs are explained for the work agreed.

Safety and preparation

Treatment advice is specific to the property. Avoid disturbing an active trail before the visit if it can be left safely; it may help locate the source.

A useful answer

Why do ants keep coming back?

A visible trail can be connected to a source that has not yet been addressed. A site assessment is needed to decide what action makes sense.
