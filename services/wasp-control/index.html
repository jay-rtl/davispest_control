Wasp control in Auckland

A wasp nest can create a serious sting risk. Davis assesses the activity and the surrounding setting before advising on the safest practical next step.

Understand the signs

What to look for

Concentrated flight paths, nest activity or repeated wasp presence near a building should be treated cautiously.

If you are unsure what you are seeing, describe the signs in your request. Avoid handling a pest or suspected nest to identify it.

The Davis approach

Assess first. Then plan.

Site assessment

Tell Davis where the activity is and whether people or pets use the area. You do not need to approach or disturb a suspected nest to describe it.

Treatment and aftercare

After discussing the findings, Davis recommends an approach suited to the pest, property and occupants. Treatment and follow-up needs are explained for the work agreed.

Safety and preparation

Keep clear of the suspected nest and follow the technician's site-specific instructions. Do not attempt to remove a nest yourself.

A useful answer

Should I block the nest entrance?

No. Disturbing or blocking a suspected nest can increase risk. Keep your distance and request professional advice.
