Cockroach control in Auckland

Cockroaches can shelter close to warmth, food and moisture. Davis starts by finding where activity is occurring and what is helping it continue, then explains a treatment plan for the property.

Understand the signs

What to look for

Droppings, egg cases, a persistent odour or activity after dark can all be useful clues.

If you are unsure what you are seeing, describe the signs in your request. Avoid handling a pest or suspected nest to identify it.

The Davis approach

Assess first. Then plan.

Site assessment

An assessment looks at likely harbourage, food and moisture sources, access and the spaces in use. Share where and when you have seen activity; that detail can help identify the problem.

Treatment and aftercare

After discussing the findings, Davis recommends an approach suited to the pest, property and occupants. Treatment and follow-up needs are explained for the work agreed.

Safety and preparation

Preparation and re-entry advice depend on the treatment selected. Ask about children, pets, food preparation areas and any other site-specific concern before work begins.

A useful answer

Should I spray the cockroaches I can see?

Visible activity may be only part of the problem. Tell Davis what you have seen before using another product so the assessment can account for it.
