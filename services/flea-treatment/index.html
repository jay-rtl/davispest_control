Understand the signs

What to look for

Bites, pet scratching or small jumping insects around floors and furnishings may indicate flea activity.

If you are unsure what you are seeing, describe the signs in your request. Avoid handling a pest or suspected nest to identify it.

The Davis approach

Assess first. Then plan.

Site assessment

Describe where activity is most noticeable and whether pets have been treated by a veterinarian. The property assessment helps determine the next step.

Treatment and aftercare

After discussing the findings, Davis recommends an approach suited to the pest, property and occupants. Treatment and follow-up needs are explained for the work agreed.

Safety and preparation

Preparation and aftercare matter. Follow the instructions given for the chosen treatment and coordinate pet care with your veterinarian.

A useful answer

Why might I still notice fleas after treatment?

The flea life cycle can affect what you notice afterwards. Davis can explain what to expect and when to follow up for the chosen treatment.
