Ongoing pest management

Move from reactive callouts to a planned programme.

Ongoing Pest Management / Maintenance Programs are available for businesses that need regular oversight and a consistent prevention focus.

  • Site inspection and pest-risk discussion
  • Treatment planning around operations and occupants
  • Scheduled pest management where appropriate
  • Prevention and maintenance recommendations
  • Discreet, professional service

A programme should reflect the pest pressure, property use and needs of the organisation — not a one-size-fits-all template.
