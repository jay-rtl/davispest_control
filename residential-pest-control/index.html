How treatment works

Assessment before action.

  1. Discuss the activity, property and occupants.
  2. Assess likely pest type, harbourage and access points.
  3. Recommend an approach suited to the situation.
  4. Carry out the agreed treatment.
  5. Provide aftercare and prevention advice.
Preparing for treatment

Instructions depend on the job.

Preparation can vary by pest, property and selected treatment. EJ will explain what needs to be moved, cleaned, covered or kept accessible before the visit.

Do not guess at re-entry times. Follow the property-specific instructions supplied for your treatment.

Safety considerations

People, pets and property in mind.

Tell EJ about children, pets, allergies, special-use rooms and any other relevant property conditions. Products and methods are selected and applied with safety, effectiveness and the treatment environment in mind.
